# Archer Brand System

The Archer brand guide is part of the engineering/design baseline. Apply these tokens and rules consistently across the public website, Admin, Driver PWA, Client PWA, and hybrid app packages.

## Brand tokens

| Token | Value | Primary use |
|---|---|---|
| Archer Black | #0A0A0A | Primary/hero background |
| Archer Orange | #E87722 | Accent, CTA, highlight, border, icon |
| Silver / Chrome | #C0C0C0 | Secondary text, dividers, premium detail |
| Pure White | #FFFFFF | Text on dark, negative space |
| Dark Gray | #2A2A2A | Body text on light backgrounds |

## Typography
- Playfair Display Bold — hero/main display headings.
- Montserrat ExtraBold — brand names, service names, buttons, labels.
- Lato Light — body, captions, descriptions, fine print.

## Visual rules
- Dark background first; black is the hero color.
- Orange is an accent, not a general fill.
- Premium, secure, elite; never trendy/playful.
- High contrast.
- Deliberate open space; avoid clutter.
- Primary logo: orange accent logo on black.
- White logo is approved on dark photography/backgrounds.
- Never place the logo on a busy image without a dark overlay.
- Never stretch, rotate, or recolor the logo outside approved brand colors.
- Minimum logo clear space: height of the “A” in Archer.

## Image direction
Preferred imagery:
- black luxury sedans/SUVs, including Cadillac Escalades
- professional drivers in black suits, white gloves, dark sunglasses
- airport tarmac/executive arrivals/door opening
- night city/bokeh cinematic imagery
- NJ/NYC skyline
- MetLife Stadium
- Teterboro Airport
- Manhattan bridges
- disciplined, professional military/law-enforcement imagery where appropriate and supported
- premium leather interiors, chrome, tinted windows

Do not make unsupported claims about certifications, legal status, security capabilities, or service guarantees.

## Brand voice
Professional, confident, security-conscious, premium but approachable, trustworthy, discreet, detail-oriented, strong/protective.

Primary slogan:
“On Time ~ In Style ~ Every Time”

Tagline:
“Arrive with Archer”

## Component guidance
Buttons:
- Use Archer Orange selectively for primary action.
- Use dark/white treatment for secondary actions.
- Preserve readable contrast.

Cards/panels:
- Prefer dark premium surfaces for key operational/premium experiences.
- Use restrained borders/dividers in silver/chrome.
- Avoid excessive rounded, playful, or consumer-app styling unless UX requires it.

Forms:
- Clear labels.
- Strong focus states.
- High contrast.
- Error states must be obvious without relying on color alone.

Responsive behavior:
- Design mobile-first.
- Do not simply shrink desktop layouts.
- Driver and client flows must be touch-friendly.
